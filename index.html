ABOUT GUBULE INITIATIVE

Who We Are

Gubule Initiative for Sustainable Development (GISD) is a community-focused development organization committed to promoting sustainable development, good governance, inclusive participation and community empowerment.

We work with communities, government institutions, development partners, civil society organizations and other stakeholders to identify community needs and support practical solutions that improve people's lives.

Our work focuses on creating opportunities for vulnerable communities, women and young people while promoting environmental protection, health, education, civic participation and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Vision

A peaceful, just and sustainable society where everyone enjoys dignity, opportunity and meaningful participation in community development.

Our Mission

To promote sustainable development, good governance, inclusive participation and community empowerment through practical and people-centered initiatives.

Our Core Values

Integrity, transparency, accountability, inclusiveness, respect, partnership, sustainability and service to communities.

Our Objectives

  • Promote good governance, accountability and civic participation.
  • Support sustainable community development initiatives.
  • Promote environmental protection and tree planting.
  • Support health awareness and community health interventions.
  • Empower women, youth and vulnerable groups.
  • Promote education, skills development and sustainable livelihoods.
  • Build partnerships with government, communities, development partners and civil society organizations.
